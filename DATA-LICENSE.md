The derivative database is offered under the ODbL 1.0, through the method in
[`ALTERATIONS.md`](ALTERATIONS.md) (section 4.6(b)).

## USDA FoodData Central

Branded Foods data from [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets) is public
domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). USDA asks to be cited as
"USDA FoodData Central". This repository holds none of its rows.
